My Small Baby (Diku) ❤️

Under the midnight sky, every firework reminds me of you ✨ Thank you for being my calm in chaos and my light in darkness. Here’s to another beautiful year together 💖.She is my small, adorable baby — cute chubby cheeks, short hair, almond-shaped eyes, and that killer expression that melts my heart 😘❤️ Her calm nature gives me peace, and her presence makes my life complete. She is the best girl I have ever met, and loving her is the easiest thing I’ve ever done. I want her support forever, and I promise to always respect her, protect her, and love her endlessly.
